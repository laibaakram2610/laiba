Our story

We started in a garage with one roaster and too much opinion about beans.

Café Laiba opened in 2011 as a weekend hobby that got out of hand. Today the roaster is bigger, but the habit hasn't changed — we still cup every new lot before it goes anywhere near a customer, and we still tell you when a batch didn't make the cut.
